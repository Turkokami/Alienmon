# Overworld sprites for the new Alienmon

On the maps (wild Alienmon you can see, and your buddy walking behind you) the new species borrow their base form's tiny sprite:
a Dunehound walks around looking like a Holopup, Bubbub (Umbra) like a plain Bubbub. This pack makes their own tiny sprites:
63 Alienmon, 8 per render, 8 renders.

## How to render
1. New ChatGPT image chat per sheet.
2. Attach `references/overworld_style.png` (three existing tiny sprites, for style and size) and that sheet's `_battle_sprites.png`
   (the 8 Alienmon to shrink, in order).
3. Paste the prompt, generate at 1024x512, save as `overworld_sheetNN.png`. I cut them up and add the little bob animation.
