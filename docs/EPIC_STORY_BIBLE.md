# ALIENMON — The Epic Story Bible

### Batch 1: The Kessler Signal

Target length: 35–40 hours for the main story, 15+ more for side stories and postgame.

Status: Phase 1 of the build plan. The location atlas (Phase 2) and the close-up map prompts (Phase 3)

are built from this document.

Canon order: this bible expands the existing quests M01–P04 and keeps their order. New main quests

are marked NEW, and new side arcs are marked ARC.

## PART 0 — The heart of the story

### Logline

A kid from a canyon town befriends a lost alien scout and follows a mysterious signal across fourteen

worlds. Along the way they learn that the creatures everyone calls Alienmon are refugees from a dead

world, that the corporation promising to "harness" their power is about to wake the thing that killed it,

and that the people who first found the Alienmon twenty years ago have been hiding the truth from

everyone, including their own families.

### Themes

Refugees and home. The Alienmon are survivors. Every world shows a different way people treat

newcomers: Ferrox puts them to work, Sporeholm studies them, Thalassa learns to share the sea

with them, Helix cages them.

Bonds over power. Thorne wants the Rift's power. The hero wins through Bond: trust between

Linker and partner.

Secrets between generations. Vashti, Teo, Thorne and Orra were the "Crater Four," the team that

reached the first seedpod. Their choices twenty years ago shape everything.

Hunger isn't evil. Oblivane, the Devourer, is the last starving remnant of something that was once

kind. The ending is about healing, not killing.

### Tone

Bright and hopeful on the surface. Each act ends on a darker reveal, and every dark reveal is answered

by friendship. Think Saturday-morning adventure with a real emotional spine.

## PART 1 — Deep lore

### 1.1 Ystra, the first world

Long before humans reached the Kessler system, the world Ystra circled a huge crimson sun on the far

side of a fold in space. Its people, the Ystrans, were tall, gentle crystal-skinned beings. They did not

conquer their world; they listened to it.

The Seedmother is Ystra's living heart: a world-tree fused with crystal that dreams new life into

being. Every Alienmon species began as one of her dreams.

The Three Pillars are her guardians:

Terravox carries forests on its back and holds the land together.

Aquelion keeps the seas and the dreams of the sleeping.

Ignarch feeds the sun's warmth into the world.

The Signal. The Ystrans spoke to Alienmon through a resonance they called the Signal: a song

between minds. Human Linker technology is a crude copy of it.

### 1.2 Oblivane, the Devourer

Oblivane was Ystra's moon-spirit, a fourth guardian whose job was to take in what was dying and

return it as starlight. When the crimson sun began to fail, the dying became too much. Oblivane took in

more and more until it could no longer let go. Its hunger grew into a void that ate light, life and finally

Ystra's sky.

Secret (revealed in P02): Oblivane still remembers being kind. It cries out in the Signal, but its voice

now sounds like hunger. The Seedmother never stopped trying to reach it.

### 1.3 The Exodus and the Relay network

As Oblivane consumed Ystra, the Ystrans made two last choices:

1. The Seedpods. The Seedmother poured her strength into thousands of crystal seedpods, each

carrying sleeping Alienmon. They were launched through the fold toward a quiet star the Ystrans

had once visited: Kessler.

2. The Relay network. Ystran engineers had built eight Relay Towers across the Kessler worlds

centuries earlier, as listening posts. They retuned them into a seal: a ring of resonance that would

hold the fold shut behind the seedpods so Oblivane could not follow. Each tower is anchored by a

living crystal, a Signal Shard, that only answers someone who shares a true Bond with an

Alienmon.

The Ystrans also left Relay Gates: walkways between worlds hidden in caves, harbors and

ruins, which wake for anyone carrying Shards.

The Ystrans themselves could not leave; they stayed to buy time. Only echoes remain: fragments of

their minds stored in crystal, which still speak in whispers across the Kessler system.

### 1.4 The Starfall (20 years ago)

The seedpods crossed the fold and fell across all fourteen Kessler worlds over a single night. Most

landed safely in remote places. The largest, the Mother Pod, came down on the edge of the system and

gouged out the Starfall crater. Where it struck, the fold is thinnest. That tear is the Rift.

### 1.5 The Crater Four (20 years ago)

Four young researchers reached the Mother Pod first:

Imogen Vashti: xenobiologist (now the hero's mentor).

Teo Reyes: explorer and pilot (Sola's father).

Kael Thorne: engineer (now Director of the Helix Directive).

Orra Venn: signal scientist (now Prime Linker).

Inside the pod they found a hatchling Zib, the Seedmother's scout, and a crystal tablet: the Ystran

Codex. Orra and Vashti decoded enough of it to build the first Linker, and the Linker League was born.

Thorne decoded a different page: the Rift as an energy source beyond anything humanity had known.

The four split over the Codex's final warning, "Never open the door; something waits behind it."

Orra and Vashti sealed the Codex in Apex Array.

Thorne copied it in secret and founded the Helix Directive.

Teo kept exploring the crater, convinced something on the other side was still alive.

The Zib hatchling slipped away into the canyons, waiting for the right Linker.

### 1.6 The last six years

Teo vanished into the crater light six years ago while chasing the Signal. Sola was seven. He

reached a floating fragment of Ystra and has survived there, bonded to a crystal Alienmon,

Lumivex.

Thorne lost his wife, Elena Thorne, to a Helix reactor accident he caused while testing Rift energy.

His secret goal: use the Rift's "infinite" energy to rewrite that moment. He tells himself it's for

humanity's power needs.

The seal is weakening. Twenty years of Helix experiments have cracked the towers. The Relay

signal pulsing across the system, the "crater pulse," is the seal straining, and a slow countdown to

Oblivane breaking through.

## PART 2 — The cast

### The hero

Name: Rook (boy) or a chosen name (girl), a silent protagonist.

From: Dustmere, Dustara.

Hidden thread: the hero's late grandmother, Nana Wren, ran the Dustmere Recovery Bay during the

Starfall and nursed the first wounded Alienmon. Her old journal, found in Part 6, shows she spoke

the Signal instinctively. Zib chose the hero for that reason.

### Zib, the Seedmother's scout

A palm-sized Volt saucer with one big eye. Zib carries a message from the Seedmother, locked inside

its core, that only opens when all eight Shards resonate.

Arc: Zib starts as comic relief: curious, glitchy, nervous.

Each Shard unlocks a "memory flash": short scenes of Ystra seen through Zib's eye.

Part 7: Zib's message plays. The Seedmother is still alive, and Oblivane is not a monster but her

lost sibling.

### Sola Reyes, the rival

Fierce, proud, funny and hurting. She wears her father's oversized jacket.

Arc:

1. She treats the hero as competition (Parts 1–2).

2. Helix promises to find her father (Part 3), and she joins them.

3. She sees caged Alienmon on Helix Station, learns Thorne called Teo "an acceptable loss," and turns

(Part 5).

4. She becomes the hero's ally, and at the crater she reaches her father first.

Her partner is the starter strong against yours. Its evolution mirrors her growth.

### Dr. Imogen Vashti, the mentor

Public: Kessler's leading xenobiologist, warm and absent-minded.

Secret: a member of the Crater Four who knew the Rift was dangerous and stayed silent to protect

the Linker League. She never told Sola what Teo was really chasing.

Arc: her guilt surfaces gradually, and she confesses at the end of Part 4 (NEW quest M10b).

### Teo Reyes, the lost father

Arc: missing for six years.

Clues: his trail is followed through Pyros, Glacia and Teo's Journal.

Rescue: Part 6. He has lived on a Ystra fragment beyond the Rift with Lumivex. He brings proof that

the Seedmother survives.

### Director Kael Thorne, the villain

Brilliant and calm. He never shouts.

Motive: grief. He believes the Rift's energy can "undo" the accident that killed Elena.

Final battle: at the crater he opens the Rift, sees Oblivane, and finally understands. His last act is to

help seal it.

Redemption: partial. He is arrested in the epilogue but sends the hero a letter from prison in the

postgame.

Signature Alienmon: Thornvoid.

## PART 3 — The full story, chapter by chapter

Each chapter lists:

Where: the sections visited (these drive the location atlas)

Quests: existing quest IDs, plus NEW quests and ARC side stories

Beats: what happens

Boss: the battles that end the chapter

Reveal: what the player learns

Map needs: any special map the chapter requires

Hours are cumulative estimates.

### PROLOGUE — "The Night the Bridge Hummed" (0–1 h)

Where: Dustmere (Your House, Vashti's Observatory Lab, Town Plaza, the Bridge).

Quests: M01, M02.

Beats:

The cold open is a dream: crystal trees, a red sun, and a voice saying "Find the one who listens."

The hero wakes in Dustmere. Mom sends them to Dr. Vashti's lab for their 13th-birthday Linker.

Sola is already there, arguing with Vashti about her father.

The hero chooses a starter, and Sola takes the strong-against one.

That night the canyon bridge hums. On the bridge, Zib appears, scans the hero, and chooses

them.

Vashti goes pale when she sees Zib, a hint she has seen it before.

Reveal: Zib is not a normal Alienmon. Vashti knows something.

## PART 6 — Key scene scripts

### S1 · Prologue dream (cold open)

Crystal trees under a dying red sun. A huge shadow swallows the stars.

VOICE: Little scout... find the one who listens. Find them before the door opens.

A small saucer-shaped light zips into the dark. Cut to: the hero waking in Dustmere.

### S2 · Zib chooses the hero (M02)

The canyon bridge at night. A low hum; the lamps flicker cyan.

DX-4: Bzzt. Frequency... friendly. Repeat. Friendly.

Zib drops from the dark, circles the hero three times, then lands on their shoulder.

ZIB: Bip!

At the observatory window across the canyon, Dr. Vashti lowers her telescope.

VASHTI (quietly): ...After twenty years. Hello again, little one.

### S3 · Sola's Choice (M08)

SOLA: Helix found Dad's signal. The same one he chased into the crater.

SOLA: You've got your Shards and your little saucer. I've got nothing but his jacket.

(Rival battle R3.)

SOLA: ...You're good. That's why I need to do this alone.

She boards the shuttle. Zib dims and turns away.

### S4 · Vashti's confession (NEW M10b)

VASHTI: Four of us found the Mother Pod. Teo, Kael, Orra and me.

VASHTI: The tablet inside said never open the door. Kael copied it anyway. Teo... Teo wanted to

knock.

VASHTI: I told Sola her father got lost. He didn't. He went looking for them on purpose, and I let

everyone think he was a fool.

VASHTI: I'm so sorry. I should have told her years ago.

### S5 · Sola turns (M15)

The Observation Deck screen plays Thorne's log.

THORNE (recording): Reyes was an acceptable loss. The data he left was worth it.

Sola rips the Helix patch from her sleeve.

SOLA: He's not data. He's my dad.

SOLA: I'm with you. Let's finish this, together.

(Double battle vs Admin Vex.)

### S6 · Zib's full message (Chapter 14)

Eight Shards orbit Zib. Its eye projects a soft hologram of the Seedmother.

SEEDMOTHER: If you hear this, my scout found a true listener.

SEEDMOTHER: The shadow that ate our sky was my sibling, our moon-heart, who took in too much

grief to bear. It is not evil. It is starving and alone.

SEEDMOTHER: The seal will not hold forever. Heal it, little ones. Heal it from home.

### S7 · Thorne at the Rift (M19)

THORNE: Do you know what infinite energy means? It means no accident is ever final.

(Final battle.)

Oblivane's eye opens in the sky. Thorne staggers.

THORNE: ...Elena isn't in there. There's nothing in there but hunger.

THORNE: Reroute the engine to the seal. I'll hold it. Go!

### S8 · Teo returns (M19)

Light folds shut. Footsteps on crystal. A tall man in a torn jacket, a crystal fox at his side.

TEO: Sola? ...You're so tall.

SOLA: You absolute idiot. (She hugs him so hard Lumivex chimes.)

### S9 · Orra's final words (M21)

ORRA: I kept the Codex locked up for nineteen years and called it protecting people.

ORRA: You carried the truth across fourteen worlds in the open. That's the difference. That's why

you're the Prime Linker now.

### S10 · Oblivane calmed (P02)

The Choir's song rises. Oblivane's thousand eyes close one by one.

ZIB: Bip... bip...

A single tear of starlight falls. For the first time in two hundred years, Oblivane lets go.
