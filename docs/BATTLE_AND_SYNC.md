# Battle abilities and Linker Sync capture

Every Alienmon has one **Trait** (a passive battle ability), a type-based moveset of up to 4 moves, and in some cases a **Signature Move** only it can learn. Wild Alienmon are captured by **Syncing**: tuning your Linker to their home frequency.

## Moves
Each of the 14 types has 4 moves: a weak attack, a strong attack, a heavy attack, and a status move. Alienmon learn them by level (1, 7, 12, 20, 30, 42, 55) from their own types. Many moves have a chance of a secondary effect.

## Status conditions
| Status | Effect | Immune types |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burn | Loses 1/16 HP each turn; physical Attack halved | Plasma, Magma |
| Poison | Loses 1/8 HP each turn | Toxin, Alloy |
| Shock | 25% chance to lose its turn; Speed halved | Volt |
| Freeze | Can't move; 25% chance to thaw each turn | Cryo |
| Confusion | 2-4 turns; 33% chance to hit itself (wears off after battle) | none |

Stat stages (Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, Speed, accuracy) go from -6 to +6 and reset on switching.

## Linker Sync capture
Alienmon are refugees, not prey, so they aren't caught by force. A Stasis Pod holds a pocket of their home frequency; your Linker has to match it.

1. Weaken the wild Alienmon. Choose **SYNC** and pick a pod.
2. A frequency bar appears. A needle sweeps across it, and a green **lock window** sits somewhere on the bar.
3. Press A to lock the signal:
    - **Perfect Sync** (needle on the yellow center line): guaranteed capture (80% for mythics).
    - **Signal locked** (inside the green window): 80% (45% legendary, 30% mythic).
    - **Signal slipped** (outside): 8%.

**Window width** grows with lower HP (up to 2.6x), a status condition (1.35x), better pods, a Friendly Signal Trait (1.4x) and Zib leading your team (1.25x). It shrinks for evolved forms, Keeper partners, legendaries, mythics and Skittish Alienmon. **Needle speed** rises with the target's level and is faster for legendaries; Singularity Pods slow it by 25%.

| Pod | Window | Needle | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stasis Pod | 1x | normal | 20 stardust |
| Harmonic Pod | 1.6x | normal | 60 stardust |
| Singularity Pod | 2.6x | 25% slower | 200 stardust |

Stardust is earned from every battle won (level x 4 for wild, ace level x 25 for Keepers) and spent at Recovery Bays. Keeper partners can't be Synced: their bond is already taken.
