## Front sheet prompt
```
Pixel-art overworld character sprites for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference sprites EXACTLY for art style, size and proportions: crisp chibi pixel art with a large head (about one third of the height), bold dark outline, soft cel shading, bright readable colours.

World: Helix Station, a space station run by the Helix organisation and Admin Vex; labs, hydroponics, captives being freed. Dress every character for this world: its climate, its colours and its jobs, so they clearly belong here.

The eight characters (all facing the viewer, a slight 3/4 front view, standing relaxed):
1. Station Engineer: an engineer in a grey jumpsuit with a toolkit and a headlamp.
2. Lab Assistant: a nervous lab assistant in a white coat with a specimen jar.
3. Hydroponics Gardener: a gardener with a spray bottle and a tray of seedlings.
4. Docking Pilot: a pilot in a flight suit with a helmet under one arm.
5. Service Droid: a friendly boxy maintenance robot on two legs with a round screen face.
6. Helix Grunt: a Helix agent in a white uniform with a dark visor and a helix emblem.
7. Freed Captive: a tired but relieved person wrapped in a thermal blanket.
8. Station Kid: a kid in a little jumpsuit floating a toy rocket in one hand.

Canvas 1536x1024, landscape. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. Eight characters in a 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells, one character per cell, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each character is FULL BODY from head to feet, centred, filling about 80% of the cell height, never touching a cell edge, with the feet on the same line near the bottom of every cell. All eight at the same scale. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no ground, no shadows, no scenery, no ball or capsule emblems.
```
