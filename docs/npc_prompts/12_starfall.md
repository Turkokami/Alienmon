## Front sheet prompt
```
Pixel-art overworld character sprites for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference sprites EXACTLY for art style, size and proportions: crisp chibi pixel art with a large head (about one third of the height), bold dark outline, soft cel shading, bright readable colours.

World: Starfall Rift, a nebula world around the Rift where the seedpods came from; Rim Camp scientists; Director Thorne runs the containment. Dress every character for this world: its climate, its colours and its jobs, so they clearly belong here.

The eight characters (all facing the viewer, a slight 3/4 front view, standing relaxed):
1. Rift Surveyor: a surveyor with an instrument backpack and a tripod scanner.
2. Seedpod Collector: a collector with a padded case holding a cracked golden seedpod shell.
3. Camp Medic: a medic with a cross armband and a first-aid satchel.
4. Meteor Prospector: a prospector with a sparkling space rock and a rock hammer.
5. Rim Camp Cook: a cook with a steaming kettle and a dented camp mug.
6. Signal Operator: an operator in a headset with a small antenna on the back.
7. Containment Officer: an officer in a dark uniform with Director Thorne's containment badge and a clipboard.
8. Star-chart Kid: a kid holding a big folded star chart, pointing up.

Canvas 1536x1024, landscape. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. Eight characters in a 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells, one character per cell, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each character is FULL BODY from head to feet, centred, filling about 80% of the cell height, never touching a cell edge, with the feet on the same line near the bottom of every cell. All eight at the same scale. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no ground, no shadows, no scenery, no ball or capsule emblems.
```
