## Front sheet prompt
```
Pixel-art overworld character sprites for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference sprites EXACTLY for art style, size and proportions: crisp chibi pixel art with a large head (about one third of the height), bold dark outline, soft cel shading, bright readable colours.

World: Ferrox, a rusty scrapyard world of cranes, railyards, forges and mines; Keeper Hank Briggs is a gruff mechanic. Dress every character for this world: its climate, its colours and its jobs, so they clearly belong here.

The eight characters (all facing the viewer, a slight 3/4 front view, standing relaxed):
1. Crane Operator: a worker in a dented hard hat and a hi-vis orange vest with a radio.
2. Scrap Picker: a scruffy scavenger with one big magnet glove and a sack of clinking parts.
3. Railyard Conductor: a conductor in a peaked cap with a whistle and a swinging signal lantern.
4. Forge Smith: a broad smith in a scorched leather apron holding a hammer, soot on the arms.
5. Rust Kid Tinkerer: a small kid with oversized goggles carrying a homemade toy robot.
6. Salvage Trader: a wiry trader in a long coat covered in pockets, gadgets dangling from it.
7. Welding Apprentice: a young welder with a welding mask flipped up and thick gloves.
8. Ferrox Foreman: a stern foreman with a big moustache, a clipboard and a pencil behind the ear.

Canvas 1536x1024, landscape. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. Eight characters in a 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells, one character per cell, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each character is FULL BODY from head to feet, centred, filling about 80% of the cell height, never touching a cell edge, with the feet on the same line near the bottom of every cell. All eight at the same scale. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no ground, no shadows, no scenery, no ball or capsule emblems.
```
