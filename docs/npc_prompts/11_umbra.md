## Front sheet prompt
```
Pixel-art overworld character sprites for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference sprites EXACTLY for art style, size and proportions: crisp chibi pixel art with a large head (about one third of the height), bold dark outline, soft cel shading, bright readable colours.

World: Umbra, a dark world of perpetual night, Gravewell town, lanterns and old mines; Keeper Mortimer Kade studies the void. Dress every character for this world: its climate, its colours and its jobs, so they clearly belong here.

The eight characters (all facing the viewer, a slight 3/4 front view, standing relaxed):
1. Lantern Lighter: a lighter carrying a ladder and a glowing violet lantern.
2. Gravekeeper: a kind old gravekeeper with a shovel and a small lantern.
3. Shadow Cartographer: a mapmaker with a glowing quill and a dark map scroll.
4. Night Market Vendor: a vendor with a tray of glowing jars of light.
5. Veiled Mourner: a quiet figure in a dark veil holding a white flower.
6. Ghost-story Kid: a kid holding a flashlight under the chin with a spooky grin.
7. Void Researcher: a researcher with dark round goggles and a humming sensor box.
8. Mine Watchman: a watchman with a miner's helmet lamp and a thick coat.

Canvas 1536x1024, landscape. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. Eight characters in a 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells, one character per cell, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each character is FULL BODY from head to feet, centred, filling about 80% of the cell height, never touching a cell edge, with the feet on the same line near the bottom of every cell. All eight at the same scale. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no ground, no shadows, no scenery, no ball or capsule emblems.
```

## Back sheet prompt (same chat, after the front)
