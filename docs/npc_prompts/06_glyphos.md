## Front sheet prompt
```
Pixel-art overworld character sprites for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference sprites EXACTLY for art style, size and proportions: crisp chibi pixel art with a large head (about one third of the height), bold dark outline, soft cel shading, bright readable colours.

World: Glyphos, a jungle world of ancient Ystran ruins, temples, murals and singing chimes; the Dig Camp studies them. Dress every character for this world: its climate, its colours and its jobs, so they clearly belong here.

The eight characters (all facing the viewer, a slight 3/4 front view, standing relaxed):
1. Archaeologist: an archaeologist in a pith helmet with a soft brush and a trowel.
2. Mural Copyist: an artist with rolls of charcoal rubbings and black-smudged fingers.
3. Chime Keeper: a calm monk in plain robes with small crystal chimes hanging from a pole.
4. Dig Camp Cook: a jolly camp cook with a ladle and a pot on a tripod strapped to the back.
5. Relic Porter: a porter with stacked wooden crates of relics on a back frame.
6. Rune Scholar: a scholar with round glasses carrying a stack of scrolls.
7. Jungle Guide: a guide with a wide-brimmed hat, a walking stick and a folded map.
8. Ruins Kid: a curious kid with a notebook full of glyph doodles and a pencil.

Canvas 1536x1024, landscape. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. Eight characters in a 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells, one character per cell, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each character is FULL BODY from head to feet, centred, filling about 80% of the cell height, never touching a cell edge, with the feet on the same line near the bottom of every cell. All eight at the same scale. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no ground, no shadows, no scenery, no ball or capsule emblems.
```

## Back sheet prompt (same chat, after the front)
```
Now draw the SAME eight characters from BEHIND (back view, as if walking away from the viewer), in the same cells and the same order, with the same outfits, colours, size and pixel style. Same canvas rules: 1536x1024, pure flat white background, 4x2 grid of 384x512 cells, full body, feet on the same line, no text, no shadows.
```
