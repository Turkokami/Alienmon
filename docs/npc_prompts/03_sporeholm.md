## Front sheet prompt
```
Pixel-art overworld character sprites for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference sprites EXACTLY for art style, size and proportions: crisp chibi pixel art with a large head (about one third of the height), bold dark outline, soft cel shading, bright readable colours.

World: Sporeholm, a misty glowing swamp of giant mushrooms and stilt villages; Keeper Dr. Lysa Moor is a botanist; the Spore Choir sings to the marsh. Dress every character for this world: its climate, its colours and its jobs, so they clearly belong here.

The eight characters (all facing the viewer, a slight 3/4 front view, standing relaxed):
1. Mushroom Forager: a forager in a mossy cloak with a basket of glowing mushroom caps.
2. Spore-mask Researcher: a scientist in a rebreather mask with sample vials on a bandolier.
3. Bog Fisher: a fisher in tall waders with a dripping net over one shoulder.
4. Swamp Herbalist: a kindly old herbalist with bundles of dried herbs hanging from her belt.
5. Firefly Lantern Kid: a kid holding a jar lantern full of glowing green fireflies.
6. Moss Ranger: a ranger wearing a cloak of living leaves and moss, a carved wooden bow-staff (no weapon pointed).
7. Stilt-house Builder: a builder with coils of rope and a plank over one shoulder.
8. Spore Choir Singer: a gentle singer in a long robe with mushroom and spore patterns, hands clasped as if humming.

Canvas 1536x1024, landscape. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. Eight characters in a 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells, one character per cell, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each character is FULL BODY from head to feet, centred, filling about 80% of the cell height, never touching a cell edge, with the feet on the same line near the bottom of every cell. All eight at the same scale. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no ground, no shadows, no scenery, no ball or capsule emblems.
```
