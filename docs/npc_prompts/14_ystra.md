## Front sheet prompt
```
Pixel-art overworld character sprites for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference sprites EXACTLY for art style, size and proportions: crisp chibi pixel art with a large head (about one third of the height), bold dark outline, soft cel shading, bright readable colours.

World: Ystra, the shattered homeworld of the Alienmon, floating fragments and ancient crystal halls; the Ystrans remember it; the Devourer and the blight threaten it. Dress every character for this world: its climate, its colours and its jobs, so they clearly belong here.

The eight characters (all facing the viewer, a slight 3/4 front view, standing relaxed):
1. Ystran Elder: an ancient elder in layered pale robes with a tall crystal staff.
2. Ystran Seedkeeper: a gentle keeper cradling a glowing seedpod.
3. Ystran Scout: a scout in light crystal-plated armour with a hooded cape.
4. Echo Child: a translucent glowing spirit child, a memory of Ystra.
5. Blight Cleanser: a cleanser with a purifying lantern that glows clean white light.
6. Ystran Artisan: an artisan shaping a floating crystal between the hands.
7. Fragment Wanderer: a traveller with a patched cloak and a walking stick topped with a crystal shard.
8. Memory Keeper: a quiet keeper holding a glowing memory crystal in both hands.

Canvas 1536x1024, landscape. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. Eight characters in a 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells, one character per cell, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each character is FULL BODY from head to feet, centred, filling about 80% of the cell height, never touching a cell edge, with the feet on the same line near the bottom of every cell. All eight at the same scale. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no ground, no shadows, no scenery, no ball or capsule emblems.
```
