# Linker Stashes

One-time item caches hidden around every world, generated by `tools/sections/build_world.py` (edit the `LOOT` and `COUNT` tables there and rebuild).
Items get better with the story: tier 1 (Dustara, Ferrox) up to tier 7 (Apex Array, Ystra). Stashes sit away from the main paths to reward exploring.
**153 stashes in total.**
