# Rift Breach tear (overworld, 4-frame loop)

- **Attach:** `references/overworld_reference.png` (for the art style)
- **Save as:** `rift_tear.png`

## Prompt
```
Pixel art for the Alienmon game, matching the attached reference screenshot's style: crisp pixel art, bold dark outlines, soft cel shading, top-down 3/4 view like the overworld maps. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background unless stated. No text, no labels, no logo, no ball, capsule or round "catch device" shapes.

A sprite sheet of a Rift Breach: a vertical tear in the air standing on the ground, like reality ripped open. Jagged edges glowing hot magenta and violet, a deep starry purple-black void inside with a bright white core line, small shards of broken space floating around it. About as tall as a person. Canvas 1024x256: four frames in a row in equal 256x256 cells, same size and position in every cell; the edges flicker and the shards drift between frames so it loops smoothly.
```
