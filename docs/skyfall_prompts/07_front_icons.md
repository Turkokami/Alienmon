## Prompt
```
Pixel art for the Alienmon game, matching the attached reference screenshot's style: crisp pixel art, bold dark outlines, soft cel shading, top-down 3/4 view like the overworld maps. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background unless stated. No text, no labels, no logo, no ball, capsule or round "catch device" shapes.

A sheet of 14 small round weather-badge icons, one per world's sky event. Each badge is a circle with a thin dark rim showing a tiny scene of the event, in that world's colours:
1. Dustara: Dust Tide, sand rolling in golden waves
2. Ferrox: Magnet Storm, scrap metal and sparks lifted by magnetism
3. Sporeholm: Spore Bloom, clouds of glowing green and violet spores
4. Thalassa: Tide Surge, warm rain over a rising sea
5. Neonis: Grid Blackout, a dark city skyline with only neon signs glowing
6. Glyphos: Glyph Wind, glowing violet runes drifting on the wind
7. Pyros: Ash Fall, hot ash and embers falling from volcanic vents
8. Glacia: Aurora Surge, a low green-and-violet aurora over ice
9. Helix Station: Bloom Cycle, station gardens bursting into leaves and flowers
10. Halo: Choir Winds, singing winds through white cloud spires, music-note ripples
11. Umbra: Shadow Front, a rolling wall of deeper darkness with star points
12. Starfall Rift: Seedpod Shower, golden seedpods streaking across a purple sky
13. Apex Array: Prism Flare, sunlight splitting into rainbow beams around crystal spires
14. Ystra: Gravity Drift, rocks floating up off the ground

Canvas 1792x512: a grid of 7 columns x 2 rows of equal 256x256 cells, in the order listed (left to right, top row first). Each badge is centered and fills about 80% of its cell. All badges the same size.
```
