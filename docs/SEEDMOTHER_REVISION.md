# Alienmon Story Revision: The Seedmother's Seeds

#### Revision 1, October 2026. This changes the core lore of Alienmon. It applies to the game, Books 1–5

#### and the anime. Where this file disagrees with the Saga Master Plan, the Story Bible or

ALIENMON_BOOK_SYNC_CHANGES.md , this file wins.

## 1. The new canon

#### Old version: The Seedmother sent seedpods away to save her children, then stayed behind on Ystra,

#### alive and slowly fading in her grove.

#### New version:

#### She split herself into the seeds. As Oblivane devoured Ystra, the Seedmother broke her whole self

#### apart into thousands of seedpods and sent them through the fold to Kessler. Every Alienmon

#### species carries a piece of her. She is not waiting anywhere; she is the Alienmon.

#### Ystra is barren. All that's left of her on Ystra is the Heartroot, one last living root in an empty crater

#### where her world-tree once stood. The Three Pillars guard it.

#### The Legend of the Unifier. The Ystrans carved a prophecy before the Exodus:

#### "She is scattered so she may live. Whoever reunites all her children will let the Seedmother grow

#### again, and her world with her."

#### What "reunite" means. It means bonding, not caging. The Unifier brings every kind of Alienmon

#### together in friendship, and their song flows into the Heartroot. Each Alienmon gives a note of her

#### song back, not its body or its life. Afterward, every Alienmon keeps living as itself.

#### Why it can't happen yet. While Oblivane still hungers, anything that grows on Ystra gets eaten.

#### Oblivane must be healed first; then the Reunion can happen.

### New motif

#### Alongside Nana Wren's line "Everybody's somebody's somebody," the saga gains a second line: "Every

#### Alienmon is a piece of her."

## 2. The villain's mirror (new)

#### Thorne and Helix have read the same prophecy, and read it wrong. They believe whoever holds every

#### species controls the Seedmother's power to regrow life. That power could bring back Elena.

#### This is why Helix catches and cages every species it can find: Project Unity, a vault containing one

#### of every Alienmon.

#### The Rift is how Thorne plans to carry that vault to Ystra and force the regrowth on his terms.

#### Theme: Helix collects by force; Rook unites by friendship. Tamsin's tutorial line from Book 1, "A

#### capture isn't taking. It's asking," becomes the heart of the saga.

## 4. Game revisions

### 4.1 Story and lore

#### Story Bible. Rewrite the Seedmother entry, the Exodus, Zib's message and the ending using Section

#### 1 of this file.

#### New key lore item: The Prophecy Mural. Found at the Glyphos Murals of Ystra. Adds the legend to

#### the in-game Codex/journal.

#### Project Unity. The Helix cage halls (Forge Fortress, Helix Station containment) are now labeled

#### Project Unity. Add one log terminal on Helix Station that explains it.

#### Dialogue to update. Any line saying the Seedmother is alive, waiting or fading changes to: she is

#### scattered in every Alienmon, and the Heartroot is fading. This affects Teo, Liss, Zib's memory

#### scenes, Orra and the Seedmother scenes.

### 4.2 The Xenodex becomes the story (new mechanic)

| Feature | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Xenodex subtitle | "Every Alienmon is a piece of her." |
| Heartroot meter | A new Xenodex page shows the Heartroot. Its glow grows with each species the player has registered as seen and bonded (caught, or befriended as story legendaries like Novaryx and Oblivane). |
| Story ending (required) | The Reunion cutscene plays when the main story ends. In it, the Keepers and every NPC trainer bring their Alienmon through the Warp Gate, so the Seedmother regrows no matter how full the player's dex is. |
| Unifier ending (bonus) | Completing the Xenodex unlocks a bonus epilogue: the player is named Unifier, walks with every Alienmon they've met under the Seedmother's tree, and gets an exclusive title and Linker skin. |
| Milestone rewards | At 50, 100, 150, 200, 250 and 300 species, a new part of Ystra blooms in the postgame and an NPC Ystran echo returns. This makes dex-filling feel like rebuilding the world. |

#### Design rule: completing the dex must never be required to see the story ending. The full Reunion

#### happens in the story; full dex completion is the reward.

### 4.3 Battles and choices

#### nothing to command (matches Book 3, Ch 21).

#### story.

#### Ending choice: keep "Both" as the canon default. The Seedmother's dialogue for each option now

#### refers to her children choosing where to live, not to her choosing.
