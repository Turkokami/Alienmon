# ALIENMON — Story & Art Reference Bible

**For ChatGPT (image generation).** Read this whole file before making any Alienmon image. It describes the game's world, story, characters, creatures, places and art style so every image you make feels like it belongs to the same game. When a request is unclear, choose the option that best matches this file.

---

## 1. The game in one paragraph

Alienmon is an original monster-collecting adventure in the spirit of the classic handheld creature-catching RPGs, but with its own world and creatures. You play a young **Linker** from the desert town of Dustmere. You befriend and capture alien creatures called **Alienmon**, battle with them, walk across colorful worlds, and fly your own starship between 14 planets of the **Kessler star system**. A villainous corporation, the **Helix Directive**, is trying to tear open a dimensional **Rift** that would unleash a planet-eating monster. The tone is bright, adventurous and warm, with a thread of cosmic mystery.

**Tone keywords:** hopeful, adventurous, cozy-sci-fi, wonder, friendship, a little spooky at the edges.

## 2. Art style rules (most important)

### 2a. Creature and character sprites
Use this style block for any Alienmon or character sprite:

> High-quality original pixel art game sprite in a modern Game Boy Advance / 16-bit monster-collecting style. Crisp, even pixel grid where every pixel is a hard square block at roughly 128x128 sprite resolution, upscaled evenly; no blur, no anti-aliasing, no painterly or noisy texture. Controlled palette of 24–32 colors with 3–4 tone cel shading, one light source from the top left, and a consistent dark 1-pixel outline around the whole silhouette. Bold, readable silhouette that still reads clearly when shrunk to 64x64. Full body visible, centered, filling about 80% of the frame, in a 3/4 view facing slightly left. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background: no shadow, no ground, no glow halo, no vignette.

- Always **full color**. Never the old green Game Boy palette.
- Keep effects (flames, sparks, bubbles) touching or very close to the body.

### 2b. Environments, towns, maps and scenes
Use this style block for places:

> Vibrant, highly detailed pixel art game environment, top-down 3/4 perspective like a modern 16-bit / 32-bit handheld RPG overworld. Saturated jewel-tone colors, crisp pixel clusters, soft glowing light sources, readable walkable paths. Clear separation between walkable ground (paths, plazas, docks, bridges) and blocked terrain (cliffs, water, lava, buildings).

**Recurring visual motifs in every settled place:**
- Cream and sandstone **dome buildings** with **glowing cyan windows and light strips**.
- Brass and gold trim.
- **Purple banners with a gold ringed-planet emblem**. This is the symbol of the Linker League and the Kessler colonies.
- Tall cyan lamp posts.
- Market stalls with **red-and-yellow striped awnings**.
- Supply crates with cyan lights.
- Lush alien plants: magenta palms, glowing mushrooms, crystal shrubs.
- Big alien skies: ringed gas giants, extra moons, floating rock islands.

**For walkable maps:** do not put the hero or random people standing in the scene unless asked. Characters are added by the game.

### 2c. Things to avoid everywhere
- Do not copy or closely resemble any existing Pokémon, Digimon or other franchise creature, character, logo or UI.
- Do not add text, captions, watermarks or UI to images unless specifically asked. Map labels are the only exception, and only when requested.
- No photorealism and no 3D render look.

## 3. World & lore

### The Starfall
Twenty years ago the sky over the Kessler system lit up. Thousands of glowing **seedpods** streaked down onto every world; this event is called the **Starfall**. Alienmon hatched from the pods.

### Where Alienmon come from
Alienmon are **refugees from the dying planet Ystra**. A cosmic void creature called **Oblivane, the Devourer**, was consuming Ystra. The ancient guardian **Seedmother** launched her children across space in seedpods to save them. The Kessler system was where they landed.

### The Rift and the Relay Towers
A tear in space, the **Rift**, connects the Starfall crater to shattered Ystra and to the Devourer. Eight **Relay Towers** across the system secretly hold the Rift shut. Each tower is guarded by a **Keeper**, and each Keeper awards a **Signal Shard**. When all eight Shards resonate together, the legendary **Novaryx** awakens.

### The villain: the Helix Directive
The Helix Directive is a sleek, ruthless research corporation led by **Director Kael Thorne**. Helix believes it can harness the Rift's power. It steals tower cores, captures Alienmon by force and builds fortress-factories.

- **Visual identity:** white and black uniforms, crimson and orange accents, visored helmets, dark steel industrial architecture with glowing orange power columns.
- **Emblem:** an **orange ring with a diagonal slash**, as seen on the Molten Frontier fortress and landing pads. Use it on Helix buildings, ships and uniforms. (Older character prompts mention a crimson double-helix; prefer the ringed slash going forward.)

### Linkers and technology
- **Linker:** a wrist-mounted handheld device that lets humans bond with Alienmon. Players are called **Linkers**.
- **Transfer Beam:** how capture works. You do not throw a ball. You fire a **cyan-white teleport beam** from your Linker, the weakened Alienmon dissolves into light, and it reappears aboard your ship's **Teleporter Bay**.
- **Beam Cells (Stasis Pods):** small glowing capsules that power the beam. There are three kinds:
  - **Stasis:** cyan, the standard cell.
  - **Harmonic:** pink-violet, stronger.
  - **Singularity:** black with a purple core, the strongest.
- **The ship:** each Linker flies a small starship, a sleek white-and-cream craft with **magenta-pink glowing engines**, a teal cockpit dome and swept fins. You can teleport to it from anywhere.
- **Recovery Bay:** a healing center. It is a dome building with a cyan cross-light, staffed by a nurse in a pink and white uniform.

## 4. Storyline (main quest)

**Act 1 — Dustara, Ferrox, Sporeholm**
- The hero leaves home in Dustmere, a canyon town on the desert world Dustara.
- Dr. Vashti gives them a starter Alienmon: Sporix, Plazmo or Gloopa.
- The little saucer Alienmon **Zib**, a scout of the Seedmother, joins them.
- The rival **Sola** takes another starter and races ahead.
- Helix agents are spotted. Shards 1–2 are won.

**Act 2 — Thalassa, Neonis, Glyphos**
- Helix attacks the harbor tower and steals its core.
- Sola, desperate to find her missing father, leaves with Helix.
- A mysterious Rift signal is discovered.
- In the ancient ruins of Glyphos, murals reveal that Alienmon fled a dying Ystra. Shards 3–4.

**Act 3 — Pyros, Glacia, Helix Station**
- In the volcanic mines the hero learns that Sola's father, **Teo Reyes**, vanished into the crater's light.
- Giant bite marks in frozen fossils show the Devourer is real.
- The hero infiltrates Helix Station and defeats Admin Vex.
- Sola turns on Helix and joins the hero. Shards 5–6.

**Act 4 — Halo, Umbra, Starfall Rift**
- Psychic twins show a vision of the Rift opening.
- In the dark city of Umbra a former Helix scientist warns of the Devourer.
- At the crater all eight Shards resonate, **Novaryx** awakens, Director Thorne is defeated and the Rift is sealed.
- Teo is rescued, now bonded with the crystal Alienmon Lumivex.

**Act 5 — Apex Array**
- The hero climbs the summit broadcast station, defeats the Quorum and Prime Linker **Orra Venn**, and becomes the new Prime Linker.

**Postgame — Ystra**
- Through the vortex, the hero explores the shattered home world of the Alienmon.
- They face Oblivane, meet the Seedmother, and choose the Alienmon's future.

**Future hook**
- A dormant **Warp Gate** at the edge of the system will open new star systems full of new Alienmon.

## 7. Alienmon design guide

### General rules
- There are 300 Alienmon in Batch 1, numbered Xenodex #001–#300. Each has 1–2 of the **14 types**.
- They should feel **alien**: unusual eye counts, glowing cores, floating bodies, crystal or plasma parts. They should still be **cute or cool and readable as a sprite**.
- **Evolution lines** (1–3 stages) grow larger, more detailed and more powerful while keeping the same core colors and one signature feature.

## 8. Battle and effect visuals

- **Battles:** the enemy Alienmon is shown upper right and your Alienmon lower left (back view). The planet's scene is the background. HP boxes are white panels with dark outlines.
- **Attack effects** are glowing pixel-art VFX matched to type. Draw them on a dark background so they can be cut out:
  - fireballs and explosions (Plasma, Magma)
  - leaf arrows and green starbursts (Bio)
  - spore clouds and lime slime waves (Toxin)
  - bubbles, waves and water-orb splashes (Hydro)
  - yellow laser beams and sparks (Volt)
  - rolling boulders (Gravity)
  - purple black-hole orbs (Void)
- **Capture:** a cyan-white Transfer Beam from the Linker locks onto the Alienmon, which dissolves into sparkling light particles.
