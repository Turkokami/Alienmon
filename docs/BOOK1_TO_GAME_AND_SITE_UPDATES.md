## Game story (quests.json / story.json)
- **Keeper teams follow the book:**
  - Hank: Girderon and Anvilox.
  - Moor: Oozel and Gloomtoad, plus Rook's new Lumislug for the player.
  - Marsh: Old Barnaby (Brineback).
  - Static: Boombastadon.
  - Dax: Rumbler.
- **Sola rival battles:** Rook loses in Ch 3 and Ch 12 and wins in Ch 19. M03 currently completes only on a win.
- **Sola's lines:** her Ch 3 line is "My dad would've laughed at you" (the game has "liked you"). In Ch 12, Gloopa evolves into Tidaloid mid-battle.
- **Helix in Act 1 (book order):**
  - **Pell and Strand in Act 1:** both appear:
    - Ch 5: tunnel ambush.
    - Ch 10: space pursuit with a tractor beam.
    - Ch 13: the Mushroom Maze siphon. Strand frees the sporeling.
    - Ch 17: the harbor core theft.
    - Ch 20: Strand alone on Neonis.
  - **Admin Vex in Act 1:** she appears in Ch 17 with her Hexcoil, before the game's M15.
  - **Tone:** serious throughout. No slapstick.
    - Pell is calm, ambitious and a true believer.
    - Strand is quiet, decent and uneasy.
    - Vex is icy, not sarcastic.
- **Helix lore:** Helix built the Linker relay grid, the radio masts and the Dustmere schoolhouse roof. Every Linker checks in with the grid, which is how Pell knows Rook's name.
- **Glacia:** it's the ringed ice world seen from space in Ch 10. The name "Ombra" is retired.
- **Kept from the book's plan, game may skip:** Sola's 2nd and 3rd battles, the Pirate Radio dish arc, and Vashti's Crater Four confession are in the book. The game can leave them out.
- **Unchanged rules:** the game still wins on types, stats and moves. Battles in the book are fierce but kid-safe, and no Alienmon dies.
