## Front prompt
```
Pixel-art creature sprite for the Alienmon game. Match the attached references exactly for art style: crisp pixel art, the same pixel size and density, bold dark outline and soft cel shading.

Design: the World-Echo form of the Alienmon "Neonmire" from Halo. It is what "Lumislug (Halo)" evolves into.
Reference 1 (Neonmire.png) is the normal Neonmire: keep its exact body shape, size, face, pose and silhouette so it is instantly recognisable as Neonmire.
Reference 2 (echo_Lumislug_Halo.png) is the Halo Echo of Lumislug, its earlier stage: copy that Echo's colours, surface and world details onto Neonmire, grown bigger and stronger to match this stage.

How the Halo Echo looks: Its glow pulses in rhythm like a speaker, its eyestalks end in little bell shapes, and its slime trail leaves faint ripples of sound.

It is Sonic/Toxin type. Pose: the same pose and facing direction as reference 1.

Canvas 1024x1024, square. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. One creature, centered, filling about 75% of the canvas, nothing touching the edges. No ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no border. No ball, capsule or round "catch device" emblem anywhere on the design.
```
