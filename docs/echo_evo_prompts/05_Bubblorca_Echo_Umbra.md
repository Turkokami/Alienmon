## Front prompt
```
Pixel-art creature sprite for the Alienmon game. Match the attached references exactly for art style: crisp pixel art, the same pixel size and density, bold dark outline and soft cel shading.

Design: the World-Echo form of the Alienmon "Bubblorca" from Umbra. It is what "Bubbub (Umbra)" evolves into.
Reference 1 (Bubblorca.png) is the normal Bubblorca: keep its exact body shape, size, face, pose and silhouette so it is instantly recognisable as Bubblorca.
Reference 2 (echo_Bubbub_Umbra.png) is the Umbra Echo of Bubbub, its earlier stage: copy that Echo's colours, surface and world details onto Bubblorca, grown bigger and stronger to match this stage.

How the Umbra Echo looks: Its bubble has turned smoky violet-black with tiny stars drifting inside, its flippers fade into wisps of shadow at the tips, and its happy grin glows faintly in the dark.

It is Void/Hydro type. Pose: the same pose and facing direction as reference 1.

Canvas 1024x1024, square. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. One creature, centered, filling about 75% of the canvas, nothing touching the edges. No ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no border. No ball, capsule or round "catch device" emblem anywhere on the design.
```
