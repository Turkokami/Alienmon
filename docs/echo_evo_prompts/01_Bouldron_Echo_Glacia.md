```

Reference 2 (echo_Pebblet_Glacia.png) is the Glacia Echo of Pebblet, its earlier stage: copy that Echo's colours, surface and world details onto Bouldron, grown bigger and stronger to match this stage.

How the Glacia Echo looks: Frost has crept into its cracks: the indigo crystals are now pale-blue ice, its brown stone is frosted white on top, and icicles hang from its edges.

It is Cryo/Gravity type. Pose: the same pose and facing direction as reference 1.

Canvas 1024x1024, square. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. One creature, centered, filling about 75% of the canvas, nothing touching the edges. No ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no border. No ball, capsule or round "catch device" emblem anywhere on the design.
```
