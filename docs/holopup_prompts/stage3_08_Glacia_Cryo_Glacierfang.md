## Front prompt
```
Pixel-art creature sprite for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference image (Holopup) exactly for art style: crisp pixel art, the same pixel size and density, bold dark outline, soft cel shading and the same glowing hologram feel.

Design: "Glacierfang", the final Cryo-type evolution of Frosthound (attached), from the ice world planet Glacia. It is a towering arctic wolf. This is the FINAL evolution. Make it noticeably bigger, stronger and more majestic than the stage-2 form, while keeping the family face, big eyes, large ears, fluffy tail shape and the round collar ring so players can tell it grew from Holopup.

Its Glacia look: Its shoulders and back are armoured in jagged blue glacier ice. A shimmering aurora (green, cyan and violet) flows from its mane like a cape. Icicle fangs, frost-rimmed fur, snow falling around it, and breath that turns to frost.

Keep the Cryo color palette of Frosthound. Pose: a proud 3/4 front standing pose, facing the same direction as the reference.

Canvas 1024x1024, square. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. One creature, centered, filling about 75% of the canvas, nothing touching the edges. No ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no border. No ball, capsule or round "catch device" emblem anywhere on the design.
```
