## Front prompt
```
Pixel-art creature sprite for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference image (Holopup) exactly for art style: crisp pixel art, the same pixel size and density, bold dark outline, soft cel shading and the same glowing hologram feel.

Design: "Scarabane", the final Swarm-type evolution of Dunehound (attached), from the desert planet Dustara. It is a tall, regal desert jackal. This is the FINAL evolution. Make it noticeably bigger, stronger and more majestic than the stage-2 form, while keeping the family face, big eyes, large ears, fluffy tail shape and the round collar ring so players can tell it grew from Holopup.

Its mane is a living swarm of hundreds of glowing sand-bugs that streams off its shoulders like a dust storm, and a ring of circling bugs forms a sun-disc behind its head. Long ears with beetle-wing edges, amber eyes, dune-ripple patterns on its legs.

Keep the Swarm color palette of Dunehound. Pose: a proud 3/4 front standing pose, facing the same direction as the reference.

Canvas 1024x1024, square. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. One creature, centered, filling about 75% of the canvas, nothing touching the edges. No ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no border. No ball, capsule or round "catch device" emblem anywhere on the design.
```
