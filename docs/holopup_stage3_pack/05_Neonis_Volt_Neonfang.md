## Front prompt
```
Pixel-art creature sprite for the Alienmon game. Match the attached reference image (Holopup) exactly for art style: crisp pixel art, the same pixel size and density, bold dark outline, soft cel shading and the same glowing hologram feel.

Design: "Neonfang", the final Volt-type evolution of Neonhound (attached), from the neon city planet Neonis. It is a sleek, fast cyber-wolf. This is the FINAL evolution. Make it noticeably bigger, stronger and more majestic than the stage-2 form, while keeping the family face, big eyes, large ears, fluffy tail shape and the round collar ring so players can tell it grew from Holopup.

Its Neonis look: Black body traced with bright neon circuit stripes in yellow, cyan and hot pink, like a city at night. Its tail is a flickering holographic billboard ribbon, a visor of light covers its eyes, and lightning arcs between its ears.

Keep the Volt color palette of Neonhound. Pose: a proud 3/4 front standing pose, facing the same direction as the reference.

Canvas 1024x1024, square. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. One creature, centered, filling about 75% of the canvas, nothing touching the edges. No ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no border. No ball, capsule or round "catch device" emblem anywhere on the design.
```
