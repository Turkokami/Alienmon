# Holopup final evolutions (stage 3): ChatGPT prompt pack

The 14 world forms (stage 2) are done. This pack makes their final evolutions.

## How to render
1. Open a **new ChatGPT image chat** for each form.
2. Attach **both** images named in the file: the stage-2 form from `references/` (the design to evolve)
   and `references/Holopup_reference.png` (the art style).
3. Paste the **front** prompt. Generate.
4. In the same chat, paste the **back view** prompt. Generate.
5. Save as `<Name>.png` and `<Name>_back.png`, one folder per form, the same way as Part 1 and Part 2.
6. Check: pure white background, whole creature visible, nothing cut off, no text, no ball or capsule emblems,
   and clearly bigger and grander than its stage-2 form.
