### Bolt Bash · ARC · save as `art/arena/raw/fx/bolt_bash.png`

> Attack effect sprite sheet for the move "Bolt Bash" (Alloy type): a heavy steel crescent swing with orange metal sparks. In the arena it is a melee swipe or bite right in front of the user.
> Canvas 1536x1024 on a fully TRANSPARENT background (if transparency is not possible, use flat pure magenta #FF00FF, a color that never appears in the effect). A 4-column x 2-row grid of equal 384x512 cells.
> Row 1: 4 frames of a crescent swipe or snapping jaws shape opening to the RIGHT, quick and punchy.
> Row 2: 4 frames of the impact burst when it hits, from a small flash to the biggest burst to fading sparks, centered in each cell.
> Colors: steel grey #8a95a5, copper #c87a3a, gunmetal #4a5260. Visual language: gears, bolts, rivets, steel plates and sparks off metal.
> Each frame is centered in its cell, and nothing crosses a cell edge except tileable beam or arc segments, which touch the left and right edges exactly. No grid lines, no labels.
> Pixel art game special effect in the same crisp 16-bit style as the Alienmon creature sprites: hard square pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no painterly texture, bold readable shapes, 3-4 tone cel shading with a bright core and a darker rim. Effects only: no creatures, no characters, no ground, no text.
