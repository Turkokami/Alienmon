# Lake maps re-render pack (all the other lakes)

Like the Ferrox reservoir: every lake map was one painting of water where you could only walk a thin shore, most entered
through a single corner tile. These re-renders give each one a walkable shore ring, bridges out to three grassy islands,
docks and tall grass, keep every existing entrance, and join each lake to its partner "Islands" map so they form a loop.

## How to render
New chat per map. Attach the **layout guide** (tile plan) and the **current art** (the world's look). Paste the prompt, 1536x1024.
It doesn't need to be pixel-perfect; I trace the walkable areas from your art. Send them in zips.
