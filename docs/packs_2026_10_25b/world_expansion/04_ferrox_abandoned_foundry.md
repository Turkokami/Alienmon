## Prompt
```
Top-down 3/4 view pixel-art game map for the Alienmon game, in the same style as the attached 04_abandoned_foundry_style.png (a neighbouring map on Ferrox): crisp detailed pixel art, the same palette and level of detail.

This is a NEW area, "Abandoned Foundry": a silent foundry yard: cold furnaces, conveyor belts, molds, rusted carts on rails, weeds growing through the floor. It should feel like a reward to find: interesting to explore, with a main path, at least one side path, open ground for wandering, two or three patches of tall grass (where wild Alienmon hide), and natural walls (cliffs, rocks, buildings or water) around the edge.

The attached 04_abandoned_foundry_entrance.png shows the only fixed rule: the red-outlined tan squares are the entrance from Railcar Maze (491). A clear walkable path must start there, on the LEFT edge of the image, and lead into the area. Everything in the light-grey part is yours to design. Each square is one 64x64 tile.

Canvas 1536x1024 (24 x 16 tiles), landscape, filled edge to edge. No characters, no creatures, no text, no UI, no grid lines, no ball or capsule emblems.
```
