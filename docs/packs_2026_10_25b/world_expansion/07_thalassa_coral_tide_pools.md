```
Top-down 3/4 view pixel-art game map for the Alienmon game, in the same style as the attached 07_coral_tide_pools_style.png (a neighbouring map on Thalassa): crisp detailed pixel art, the same palette and level of detail.

This is a NEW area, "Coral Tide Pools": shallow turquoise tide pools on a reef at low tide, coral, starfish, rock paths between pools, sand bars with tall sea grass. It should feel like a reward to find: interesting to explore, with a main path, at least one side path, open ground for wandering, two or three patches of tall grass (where wild Alienmon hide), and natural walls (cliffs, rocks, buildings or water) around the edge.

The attached 07_coral_tide_pools_entrance.png shows the only fixed rule: the red-outlined tan squares are the entrance from Driftwood Hollows (783). A clear walkable path must start there, on the TOP edge of the image, and lead into the area. Everything in the light-grey part is yours to design. Each square is one 64x64 tile.

Canvas 1536x1024 (24 x 16 tiles), landscape, filled edge to edge. No characters, no creatures, no text, no UI, no grid lines, no ball or capsule emblems.
```
