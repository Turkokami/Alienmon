# Alienmon Book 1 — Character Render Prompts (game style)

Every character in *Alienmon: The Kessler Signal* as a copy-paste prompt for a game character sheet. Each prompt is built from the book's descriptions (book wins on looks; where the book is silent, the current game sprite's look is kept) and the game's existing pixel-art style.

## How to use
1. Paste one character's **Prompt** into ChatGPT image generation (landscape, 1536×1024). The prompt already contains the full style block and the exact sheet layout.
2. Check the result: white background, bust top-left, full body top-middle, 4×4 walk grid top-right, poses and faces along the bottom, nothing touching. Regenerate if the grid is uneven.
3. Save it as `art/raw/<key>_sheet.png` and import it:
   - Rook (player): `python tools/import_character_sheet.py art/raw/boy_sheet.png boy --player`
   - Everyone else: `python tools/import_character_sheet.py art/raw/<key>_sheet.png <key>`
   - Add `--holes` only for characters with gaps in their hair and no white clothing (never for Helix, Vashti or anyone in white).
   This writes the 64 px walk frames, the 128 px (160 px for Rook) overworld sprite and the 384 px dialogue portrait.
4. The **Optional detailed render** under each prompt is a single 1024×1024 high-detail bust/portrait for dialogue art, the website or book promos.
5. Ystra (the Seedmother vision) and the Crater Four photo are key-art prompts, not sheets.

Render priority 1 first: they appear in Book 1 story scenes. Characters marked **replace** have a game sprite today that doesn't match the book (Kit, Hank and Strand most of all).

## Notes that apply to every sheet (from reading `tools/import_character_sheet.py`)
- Every walk cell and the full body are scaled **by height** (walk frames to 60 px, full body to 124/156 px). So: the figure must be the same height in every walk frame (no up-and-down bob, no raised arms or props poking above the head in the grid), and nothing in a walk frame may be wider than it is tall.
- The cutter treats any near-white (all channels above about 226) touching the outside as background. Every pale colour below is kept at or under about #E8E0D0, and every figure has a closed dark outline. Do not let ChatGPT "lighten" pale clothing to pure white.
- Small detached bits (sparkles, sweat drops, glow pixels floating free) are dropped by the cutter. Keep glows attached to their object.
- Row 4 of the grid is not imported; it is a spare copy of row 3. Row 1 cell 3 and row 2 cell 3 are also unused (repeat standing frames).
- Alienmon partners appear only in the BOTTOM STRIP, never in the walk grid or the full body (they have their own sprites), except where noted for `rockgirl`.
- Teo's olive jacket and Sola's olive jacket are the **same jacket**. Both prompts give it the same small faded tan star patch on the left upper sleeve and the same three dark brass buttons, so readers can see it pass from father to daughter.
