# Dustara art direction (Dustmere and the Observatory)

What the graphic-novel pages show, written as rules for building the 3D game's Dustara scenes.
Sources: `sporeholm3d/art/gn/` (E01, E02, E03 pages; the key ones are named below), plus the Dustara town prompt in
`docs/MAP_AND_PLANET_PROMPTS.md`. When a scene and this note disagree, the pages win.

## 1. Palette

| Role | Colour | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Stone and walls | warm cream to honey sandstone, `#e8c890` to `#d9a86a` | houses, balustrades, stair slabs, plaza paving |
| Cliffs | banded red-orange, `#c4532e` / `#e07a45` / `#f0a868`, with pale sandy stripes | every horizon (E03 P02-P15) |
| Trim | warm brass and gold, `#c9973a` to `#e8c060` | telescopes, door frames, lamp caps, rails |
| Glow | turquoise / cyan, `#3fd6d0` to `#7ff0ff` | windows, lamp slits, dome bands, light strips |
| Banners | royal purple `#5a2c8a` with a gold ringed-planet emblem | posts, stair tops, the plaza |
| Accents | red-and-yellow striped awnings; blue-and-white pottery | market, kitchens, planters |
| Plants | sage and olive green, small pink and yellow blooms | cypress, ivy, cactus gardens |
| Sky | day: clear blue with soft white clouds. Morning and evening: pink-lilac to peach, two small moons | E01 P18-P20, P44 |

Keep saturation high but warm. Nothing in a settled place is grey: shadows go terracotta or violet, never black.

## 2. Light

- Low, warm sun from one side, long soft shadows, strong rim light on domes and rails.
- Interior and window light is cyan; firelight and lanterns are amber. Both show at once at dusk.
- Haze thickens with distance and turns peach, so the far cliffs read as flat warm bands (fog colour near `#e8b890`).
- Night: violet sky, cyan window glow, blue static sparks in the drift grass.

## 3. Terrain shapes

- Mesas and buttes with flat tops and vertical, banded faces, stepped like layered cake (E03).
- Canyons crossed by rope-and-steel bridges; lava falls in the deep cuts.
- Slopes read as bands: a red layer, a pale sandy layer, a red layer. Loose scree and a few boulders at the foot, not everywhere.
- Paths are packed sand with sandstone kerbs; important routes get carved stairs with a pale, worn lip on each step.

## 4. Building style

- Cream sandstone domes and drums, thick walls, round and arched windows with brass frames and cyan glass.
- Gold-trimmed arched doors with teal glass panels (the Observatory door, E01 P27).
- Small domed turrets and lantern pillars mark anything important.
- Interiors: stone walls, brass instruments, bookshelves, star charts, ivy (E01 P24-P30).
- Market and corral pieces are wood: rail fences, water towers, windmills, striped awnings (E03 P09-P15).

## 5. The Observatory (E01 P44 and P52 are the target)

- It sits far above the town on its own mesa, reached by a long climb, so it looks down on all of Dustmere.
- A grand straight stair with sandstone balustrades leads to the courtyard.
- At the top: a white dome with a turquoise band and a roof slot, a great brass telescope, the gold door with teal glass.
- Lamp pillars line the stair and courtyard: sandstone shafts, brass caps, turquoise light slits.
- Purple ringed-planet banners on poles; dark green cypress trees in pairs; small domed turrets at the edge.
- A balcony with a brass rail looks out over the bridge and the town (P52).
- Sky behind it: pink-lilac with two moons.

## 6. Vegetation

- Saguaro and prickly pear in clusters of 2-4, never in even rows.
- Golden drift grass in patches on open flats.
- Cypress only at built, important places (the Observatory); ivy and potted flowers on houses.
- Keep slopes and viewpoints mostly clear: a few plants that frame the view, not a carpet.

## 7. Scale

- Rook is about 1.7 m. Doors 2.2-2.6 m. Ordinary houses one or two storeys; domes 6-9 m across.
- The Observatory dome is the biggest building in the region, about 11 m across, and its summit sits about 26 m above the town.
- Paths are 2.5 m wide; stairs at least as wide as the path that reaches them.

## 8. Sightlines

- From the plaza and the bridge you should see the Observatory high on its mesa: it is the goal of the first walk.
- From the Observatory balcony you should see the whole town, the bridge and the far cliffs.
- Every climb ends in a view. Put benches, telescopes or a rail where the view opens.
- Keep tall props off the sightline between a viewpoint and what it looks at.

## 9. What was applied in the Nov 24 Observatory move

- The Observatory moved from the town hill to Starfall Mesa, east past the bridge, up five rope-railed switchbacks and a
  grand stair, 26 m above the town.
- Summit built to P44: white dome with a turquoise band, brass telescope, gold door, lamp pillars, purple banners,
  cypress, turrets, a star-map mosaic in the courtyard.
- West balcony built to P52: brass rail, bench and two coin telescopes aimed at the plaza and the bridge.
- Banded red slopes with sandstone kerbs and cairns; rocks and plants thinned on the climb and the balcony so the views stay open.
- Low-detail stand-ins of the town show from the summit, so the balcony really looks down on Dustmere.

Not yet applied (later map passes): the pink-lilac two-moon sky at the summit, the town's striped awnings and water
towers matched to E03, and the corral's wood details.
