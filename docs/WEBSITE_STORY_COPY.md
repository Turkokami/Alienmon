## 2. Story page (/story): full rewrite and expansion

### Intro
**The Alienmon Chronicles**
A space adventure about friendship, courage and listening, told across a game, an animated series and a five-book saga by Kristofer Elling. Everything below is safe to read before you start: no spoilers past the first chapter.

### The Starfall
Twenty years ago, on a still and moonless night, the sky over the desert world of Dustara caught fire. One light, then ten, then a hundred, until the whole sky rained stars of gold, violet and green.

The stars didn't burn up. They landed softly as glowing seedpods on all fourteen worlds of the Kessler system, and from each one hatched a creature no one had ever seen: Alienmon. Some were small enough to sit on your shoulder. Some were bigger than a house. All of them were a little lost.

The largest pod of all struck the canyon country outside Dustmere and left a crater that still glows purple at night. People call it the Starfall Crater. Every so often, it pulses.

### Ystra, the world they fled
The Alienmon didn't come from nowhere. Carvings on the ruin-world of Glyphos tell of Ystra, a world of crystal under a dying crimson sun, and of its people, the Ystrans, who listened to their world through a song between minds called the Signal.

At the heart of Ystra stood the Seedmother, a living world-tree whose dreams became every kind of Alienmon. When something began to swallow Ystra's sky, the Seedmother sent her children away to safety, across a fold in space, to Kessler.

What swallowed the sky? The murals only call it the Devourer.

### Linkers and the Transfer Beam
In Kessler, people and Alienmon learned to live side by side. Linkers wear a Linker on the wrist, a device that tunes into an Alienmon's signal. Weaken a wild Alienmon in battle, lock on to its frequency, and the cyan-white Transfer Beam carries it safely aboard your ship. No cages, no balls to throw: a Linker bonds with a partner, and the partner chooses to stay.

On their thirteenth birthday, kids in Dustmere climb the 112 steps to Dr. Vashti's Observatory to meet their first partner. Her only advice: "Choose with your heart."

### Relay Towers and Signal Shards
Eight ancient Relay Towers stand across the Kessler system, built long before anyone in Kessler arrived. Each is guarded by a Keeper, the strongest Linker on that world, and anchored by a Signal Shard that only answers a true bond.

Win a Keeper's respect and you win their Shard. Win all eight and something old wakes up. Nobody alive knows exactly what the towers were built to do. A few people are starting to guess.

### Zib
On the night of Rook's thirteenth birthday, the ancient bridge below Dustmere starts to hum, and a palm-sized saucer with one enormous eye drops out of the sky and chooses him. No database on any world knows what it is. It says only one word, "Bip," and its lights change color with its mood: gold when happy, purple when grumpy, blue when nervous.

Zib is carrying something. Every so often, a memory flickers across its eye: a red sun, a crystal world, a song. Zib has a message to deliver, and it has chosen Rook to help.

### The Helix Directive
The Helix Directive is the biggest company in the Kessler system: clean white labs, glowing logos, and agents in white coats who are "just collecting samples." Its Director, Kael Thorne, is brilliant, calm and very, very patient.

Helix has been studying the Relay Towers for fifteen years. Ships circle the towers at night. Keepers report strange cracks. And the Director has a plan with a name that nobody outside Helix is supposed to hear: Phase Three.

### The heroes
**Rook.** Thirteen, fast, brave, and a little too quick to rush in. Rook grew up in Dustmere hearing stories about the Starfall. He never expected to become one. His lesson for the whole saga starts in Book 1: think first, then act.

**Sola Reyes.** Rook's rival, and the best young Linker in Dustmere. Sharp, proud and always one step ahead. Her father, the explorer Teo Reyes, walked into the crater's light six years ago and never came back. Sola will do anything to find him, and "anything" might mean the wrong side.

**Kit Briggs.** The team's engineer. Kit can fix anything with a wrench, some wire and a stubborn grin, and has a habit of being the last person they think to look after. Kit's partner is Sporix, the shy pod nobody else picked.

**Dr. Imogen Vashti.** Dustmere's xenobiologist, keeper of the Observatory, and the person who hands every new Linker their first partner. Warm, brilliant, and holding onto a secret that is twenty years old.

**The Keepers.** Eight Linkers guarding eight towers: Hank Briggs of Ferrox, Dr. Lysa Moor of Sporeholm, Captain Delia Marsh of Thalassa, DJ Static of Neonis, Old Basalt of Pyros, Ingrid Vale of Glacia, the twins Echo and Iris of Halo, and Mortimer Kade of Umbra. Each one tests a different kind of courage.

### What the saga is about
Alienmon is about the creatures, and the people who love them. Battles are puzzles won by thinking, teamwork and knowing your types, never by shouting louder. Villains have reasons. Every dark secret is answered by an act of friendship. And underneath it all is one question the saga keeps asking: what does it really mean to listen?

## 3. The five books (new section on /story, or a /book index page)

### Book 1 · The Kessler Signal
*A scout falls from the sky and chooses a boy.*

On his thirteenth birthday Rook meets his first partner, and that same night a tiny saucer named Zib drops out of the sky and chooses him. Nobody knows what Zib is. The Helix Directive wants to find out. To follow Zib's signal, Rook must cross six worlds, earn his first Signal Shards from the Keepers, and face his rival Sola again and again. The murals of Glyphos hold a warning, and on Dustara, deep in the crater, something has started to wake.
**Worlds:** Dustara, Ferrox, Sporeholm, Thalassa, Neonis, Glyphos · **Shards:** 1–4 · **Status:** written. Read Chapter 1 free, or listen to the audiobook preview.

### Book 2 · The Eighth Tower
*Four Shards left, and every one is a choice.*

The race for the last four Shards takes Rook through fire, ice and the white halls of Helix itself. New Keepers, old ruins and a friend in trouble push him further than he's ever gone. Zib remembers more with every tower, and some memories are hard to carry. When the eighth tower finally answers, Rook must decide whether winning is the right thing to do.
**Worlds:** Pyros, Glacia, Helix Station, Halo, Umbra · **Status:** in development.

### Book 3 · Phase Three
*Nine days to hold the sky together.*

Phase Three begins. For nine days, every world in the Kessler system is under siege, and Rook can't win this one by battling alone. He'll need every Keeper, every friend and every partner he's ever made, and a way down into the Starfall Crater, where the story began.
**Worlds:** Starfall Rift, and every Keeper world under attack · **Status:** in development.

### Book 4 · The Summit
*Home holds the truth no one wanted to say.*

Back in Dustmere, life almost feels normal: dust-bike races, a burned pie, a house that isn't empty anymore. But the grown-ups have kept secrets for twenty years, and it's time they came out. At the top of the Apex Array waits the greatest Linker who ever lived, and a test Rook can't pass by being strong.
**Worlds:** Dustmere, Apex Array · **Status:** in development.

### Book 5 · The Seedmother's Song
*The journey ends where it all began.*

A door opens to the one place no one in Kessler has ever been: Ystra, the world the Alienmon fled. Its guardians are waiting, its echoes are listening, and somewhere in its broken sky is the reason the stars fell. The final chapter of the Alienmon Chronicles asks Rook the hardest question of all, and the answer isn't a battle.
**Worlds:** Ystra · **Status:** in development.

## 4. Worlds page (/worlds): one paragraph per world

Show on each world card, and as the intro on its world page. Keepers and types follow the game data.

**Dustara.** A sunset world of red mesas, cactus gardens and golden drift grass that crackles with tiny blue sparks. Rook's hometown of Dustmere sits on the canyon rim beside the Starfall Crater, which still glows purple after dark. Dr. Vashti's Observatory, the Trading Post and the Molten Frontier beyond the tunnels make Dustara the gateway to the whole system. *No Keeper. Every journey starts here.*

**Ferrox.** A rusting scrapyard world of cranes, railcars and mountains of metal, where sparks fly day and night. The people of Rustvale build everything out of salvage, including half of their Relay Tower. *Keeper: Hank Briggs, Alloy. His Alienmon hit like freight trains.*

**Sporeholm.** Glowing fungal swamps, lantern marshes and mushrooms taller than towers. The spores here drift toward the Relay Tower, almost as if they're homesick. *Keeper: Dr. Lysa Moor, Toxin. Patient, precise and never in a hurry.*

**Thalassa.** A ringed ocean world of coral reefs, sea arches and busy wooden docks. Port Saltreach smells of salt and engine oil, and lately Helix ships have been circling the tower at night. *Keeper: Captain Delia Marsh, Hydro. The tide waits for no one.*

**Neonis.** A city of neon, music and broadcast towers that never sleeps. Arcades buzz, speakers thump, and somewhere in the static a strange signal from the Starfall Rift repeats every eight seconds. *Keeper: DJ Static, Volt. Turn it up.*

**Glyphos.** A world of sunken ruins, coral-covered temples and glowing glyphs older than anyone can count. Its murals hold the oldest story in the system, if you can read it. *No tower Keeper; the ruins themselves have something to say.*

**Pyros.** A volcanic world of lava rails, deep mines and furnace ridges. The miners here remember the night the first pod landed, and the explorer who walked into its light. *Keeper: Old Basalt, Magma. Sixty years in the mines.*

**Glacia.** Aurora skies, crystal caverns and frozen falls. Its researchers dig fossils out of the ice, and some of them have bite marks the size of buildings. *Keeper: Ingrid Vale, Cryo. Keep your cool.*

**Helix Station.** The gleaming orbital headquarters of the Helix Directive: docking rings, containment wings and laboratories nobody is supposed to see. Visitors are not welcome. *Run by Admin Vex.*

**Halo.** Floating islands, wind bridges and sky temples drifting through pink clouds. The twin Keepers are said to see the future, and lately they've stopped smiling. *Keepers: Echo and Iris, Psi.*

**Umbra.** A shadow world lit only by lanterns, where streets wind down into a sinkhole with no visible bottom. The Keeper here used to work for Helix and quit without saying why. *Keeper: Mortimer Kade, Void.*

**Starfall Rift.** A nebula-wrapped world of seedpod fields and crater rims, where the sky shimmers like something is pressing against it from the other side. *The edge of everything.*

**Apex Array.** A vast observatory on the highest peak in the system, home of the Linker Quorum and the Prime Linker, the most respected Linker alive. *Only the best are invited.*

**Ystra.** The world the Alienmon came from. No one in Kessler has ever seen it. *Coming in the final chapter of the saga.*

## 5. Game page addition: "A story you play"
**A story you play.** The game follows the saga from Rook's thirteenth birthday onward: 23 story missions, 48 side quests, eight Keepers, a rival who never lets you rest, and a mystery that grows with every Shard. Catch and bond with 300 Alienmon across 14 worlds, each living in habitats that suit them, from desert canyons to undersea vents. Your choices, your team and your Xenodex are yours. The story belongs to everyone who listens.
