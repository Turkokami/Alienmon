ALIENMON — DUSTARA CHARACTER T-POSE TURNAROUNDS
57 people, NPCs and DX-4; 228 total views.

View order in every PNG: FRONT, LEFT-FACING PROFILE, BACK, RIGHT-FACING PROFILE.
Each sheet contains four full-body views at matching scale on white.
Profile arms are foreshortened end-on in the same T-pose.

The original character references govern identity and clothing. The rendering
style follows the earlier Sporeholm drawn turnaround sheets. Original pixel
references have been translated into clean cel-shaded drawings. Where no
original side or rear reference exists, unseen construction is inferred.
Handheld equipment is stowed or omitted to keep the arms in neutral T-pose.

DX-4 correction: ordinary articulated brass robot hands with palms, fingers
and thumbs in all four views. Brooms are separate tools, never his hands.

Roster: 001–008 Dustmere story cast; 009–022 official Dustara townsfolk;
023–057 the 35 characters in the original Dustara expansion NPC pack.
Creatures are outside this people/NPC set.

Every ZIP contains its PNGs, a batch manifest, the full roster, and the
generation/correction prompts for its sheets. Files are ordered by ID.
